Issues to address first

These are code observations and a local reproduction, not reports of failures observed in the live deployment. No application fixes were made during this audit.

P0 Delivery-to-invoice drops sales charges and source currency

The invoice builder totals delivery-line prices less discounts, then sets tax_amount=0 and total_amount=total_amt. It uses the tenant currency rather than carrying the quotation currency and exchange-rate context. Its journal has only receivable and revenue legs. A taxed or shipping-bearing order can therefore produce an invoice with a smaller total, and a foreign-currency quote loses its currency context.

Required: define partial-delivery allocation for tax and shipping, persist invoice snapshots, preserve document/base currency, and post the corresponding tax, charge and exchange-rate legs. Reconcile the sum of partial invoices to the order with rounding rules.

commercial.py:1232–1340 · invoice construction and postingsales.py:297 · invoice schema

P0 Sales-team invoice reference is used before it exists

The route constructs an invoice and calls invoice_sales_team_snapshot(..., invoice.invoice_id) before adding/flushing that invoice. The SQLAlchemy UUID default has not run at that point. A local, database-free reproduction returned invoice_id_before_flush=None and sales_team_document_id_before_flush=None; the child document reference is non-nullable. This makes partner-assigned invoicing likely to fail at persistence.

Required: allocate the invoice ID before constructing children, or flush the invoice first inside the same transaction. Add a route-level test using a real database; the helper tests currently pass a pre-existing UUID and do not exercise this route order.

commercial.py:1249–1283sales_team_service.py:9phase_b.py:136

P0 Supplier “settled” balance is inferred from matching/posting

The supplier ledger summary counts MATCHED and POSTED invoices as settled. Matching an invoice to a PO/receipt does not prove payment. Both party summaries aggregate invoices rather than journal entries, so they do not provide opening balances or a transaction-by-transaction running balance.

Required: party dimensions in the GL, payment/adjustment attribution, and journal-based balances. Until then, describe these endpoints as invoice-status summaries.

enterprise_reports_service.py:202 · party summariesledger.py:24 · no party dimensions

P0 Returns and FIFO do not yet preserve the full stock-cost history

Migration 043 creates FIFO layers and an insert trigger that consumes them under locks. It does not add cancellation/update reversal handling. Credit-note restocking uses the returned invoice quantity directly and values the stock entry at the sales unit price, without applying the order's UOM factor or restoring the original consumed cost layers.

Required: convert return quantities to stock units, restore original costs/layers, implement cancellation and as-of reversal behavior, and reconcile stock quantity and value after issue → return → cancellation sequences.

043_persist_stock_fifo_layers.py:68–127returns_service.py:180–205

P1 Sales amendments omit part of the original commercial snapshot

The amendment builder copies header totals but omits the tax snapshot, shipping amount and calculation hash. It also copies line prices/totals without the line discount, promotion/free-item metadata or sales team. A discounted or promoted amended order can therefore retain one headline total while downstream calculation uses incomplete line context.

Required: define a complete snapshot-copy contract and test amendments of discounted, taxed, shipping-bearing and free-item orders, including subsequent delivery/invoicing.

commercial.py:934–958 · amendment header/line copy

P1 Adjacent reports can misstate totals in richer scenarios

Projected stock subtracts both reserved quantity and open sales demand without explaining their overlap and sums entered commercial units. Sales analytics joins every sales-team assignment without multiplying revenue by contribution, so several partners can duplicate revenue. The finished-goods subcontract report aggregates receipts by order and item rather than source order-line ID, so duplicate finished-item lines can repeat receipt quantities.

Required: define the projection formula, normalize units, allocate partner revenue or deduplicate it, and introduce explicit subcontract receipt-line lineage.

Shared requirements beyond individual features

These requirements apply across the inventory; they are not additional feature counts.

1. Consistent document lifecycle

PO lifecycle endpoints exist; sales orders have confirm/cancel/amend, but there is no matching general draft-update/recalculate contract across all document families. Bring all conversion entry points under the same validation, locking, snapshot and progress rules. Verify amendments copy every calculation and source field.

Done when: submitted values cannot change through any supported endpoint, amendments preserve the original, and retries do not duplicate movements or journals.

2. Source lineage throughout sourcing

The sourcing award path resolves RFQ lines through an item-ID dictionary. RFQ and supplier-quote schemas do not provide the full source-line chain from a material request. Duplicate item lines and unlinked RFQs need explicit IDs and an exception bucket.

Done when: MR → RFQ → supplier quotation → PO → receipt → invoice works for duplicate items and one-to-many splits without item/date inference.

3. Tenant and document ownership

Many new lookups are tenant scoped. Complete negative tests for every generation and lookup path, including inactive masters, wrong party/address, polymorphic document references and warehouse ownership. Generic SalesTeam CRUD checks contribution totals but does not enforce the referenced document's submitted lifecycle.

Done when: cross-tenant IDs and unauthorized edits to posted document children fail without side effects.

4. Architecture record and tenant flags

The plan names an architecture decision record and flags for commercial calculation, loyalty, reporting and leave control. The named ADR and those flag identifiers were not found in application code. Tenant operational settings exist, but do not implement the planned rollout gates.

Done when: decisions are documented, features can be enabled per tenant, and the disable path preserves posted history.

5. Migration and historical-data acceptance

Additive revisions 039–056 exist, with 056 as the current local head. PostgreSQL acceptance exercises selected revision upgrade/downgrade operations; a full fresh-database and prior-revision upgrade, live-data reconciliation, and deployed-revision check remain outstanding. FIFO opening layers are marked OPENING_BALANCE, but their migration-time quantities do not reconstruct pre-upgrade history.

Done when: fresh and revision-038 databases upgrade in staging, existing data reconciles, constraints are checked and legacy estimates/unlinked records are visible.

6. Durable accounting and valuation invariants

Supplier-invoice tax math and balanced-posting helpers have focused tests. Extend actual journal/stock reconciliation to sales tax, shipping, FX, commission, loyalty, returns and subcontracting. Existing balanced-proposal fixtures are not a full persisted-transaction acceptance harness.

Done when: each posted event balances, retries post once, and reversal pairs restore party balances and stock values.

7. Report contract and presentation

Enterprise reports share a generic table UI. Add schema versions, bounded date-range validation, pagination, appropriate filters, CSV exports, saved filters, charts, definitions, source links and visible legacy-estimate labels. The UI still calls FIFO ageing an estimate even though new persisted-layer reporting exists.

Done when: large results remain usable and drill-down/export totals reconcile to source documents and GL/stock.

8. Production-shaped acceptance and rollout

Run purchase-to-pay, quote-to-cash, POS, returns, transfer, subcontracting and leave-control scenarios against a disposable/staging database and browser. Add reconciliation checks, support instructions, a tenant pilot and monitoring before broad enablement.

Done when: acceptance evidence, release checklist, deployed revision and feature enablement are recorded together.

Scope, counting and confidence

This is an audit of the remaining-work plan against the local code, not an upstream ERPNext feature census. It covers Accounting, Buying, Selling, related Stock features and the HR leave gap called out in the plan. Other modules listed in the original outline—such as Assets, Payroll, Manufacturing, Projects and Support—were not fully re-audited here; previous “implemented” labels for those areas are not a new certification.

The plan headline says “35 partial + 1 missing = 36.” Its actual mapping has 4 Accounting bullets, 12 Buying bullets, 12 Selling/Stock bullets and 2 HR bullets: 30 grouped entries. Splitting Item Price from Price List and the combined invoice/quotation/order/delivery trend entries yields 34 distinct requirements. The headline cannot be reconciled to its own mapping without adding or duplicating scope. This report uses explicit IDs F01–F34 so future completion counts have a stable denominator.

Statuses reflect implementation depth, not equal units of effort. No feature is counted as accepted end to end merely because a model, route or unit test exists. “Alternative implemented” records the plan's allowed rename of supplier-wise sales analytics to supplier sourcing; it does not claim supplier-attributed revenue.
