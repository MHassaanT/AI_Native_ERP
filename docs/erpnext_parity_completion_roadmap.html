Release gate

What each workstream completes

Commercial documents and procurement

Make every conversion traceable, and every commercial field explicit.

  • Material Request details, terms and external metadata
  • Purchase Order contacts, addresses, taxes, terms, shipping, UOM, discounts and source lines
  • Sales Order taxes, terms, addresses, source quotation and fulfilment/billing progress
  • Procurement Tracker and item purchase history based on real source links
  • Subcontract settings plus transfer/subcontract reports

Pricing, taxes, UOM and fulfilment

Use one backend calculation service for all drafts; persist its result at submission.

  • Item Price and Price List selection
  • Pricing Rules, Promotional Schemes and Product Bundles
  • Purchase tax, shipping rule and Terms template snapshots
  • Naming Series atomic allocation
  • UOM conversion in purchase, sales, inventory and manufacturing lines

Sales incentives

Turn configuration-only masters into operational behavior.

  • Sales partner/team assignment, contribution validation and commission event
  • Loyalty enrollment, earn, redemption, expiry and cancellation/return reversal
  • POS loyalty and price calculation parity with Sales Invoice

Reports that can be trusted

Replace estimates and inference with persisted accounting and stock facts.

  • Customer and Supplier ledger journals with running balance
  • Recorded gross-profit cost snapshots
  • Persistent FIFO stock layers for exact ageing
  • Stock summary, transfer reports, exact projected quantity
  • Chart, drill-through and export experience for all trends

HR leave administration

Complete the only missing function and the related partial leave-period capability.

  • LeavePeriod model and allocation/policy linkage
  • Leave Control Panel preview and bulk allocation batches
  • Safe employee filters, carry-forward checks and idempotent retries

Data safety and release discipline

Protect history and make the new behavior reversible operationally.

  • Additive Alembic revisions only; no edits to 032–038
  • Tenant-scoped lookups and ledger-balance invariants
  • Feature-flagged pilot deployment
  • Legacy data remains labelled estimated/unlinked instead of guessed

Release acceptance gates

Correctness

Submitted records retain snapshots; conversion lineage is navigable; tenant isolation and double-entry invariants pass.

Data quality

Legacy data is never silently inferred. Reports flag estimated cost, FIFO, or unlinked history.

Operational readiness

Staging reconciliation, pilot feature flags, monitoring, error runbooks, and a safe disable path are complete.

Recommended first release

Implement Phases 1 and the core of Phase 2 first: document lineage, contact/address/terms snapshots, UOM persistence, price resolution, and tax snapshots. This resolves the most partial features and establishes the trustworthy data needed for loyalty, procurement tracking, margin, stock ageing, and reports.
